Privacy

Last updated 28 November 2026

Pitwall (pitwall.company) is an unofficial, independent fan project. We collect as little as we can, we never sell anything about you, and there are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who runs it

Pitwall is made by Ahmed Masoud. Questions or requests about your data: ahmoudy365@gmail.com.

Usage counts (everyone)

To see how the site is used, it records anonymous events: which screens are opened, when a prediction is generated or shared, and errors. With each event we keep the country (worked out from your IP address, which is then thrown away), the type of device, the operating system and browser, the language, the app version and the website that sent you here, if any.

To count visitors without cookies, the server makes a one-way code from your IP address and browser, mixed with a secret that changes every day. The code cannot be turned back into your IP address, and it changes the next day, so you can be counted for one day but never followed over time. Nothing is stored on your device for this.

If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control”, none of this is recorded. Raw events are deleted after 35 days; after that only daily totals remain (for example “120 visitors from Spain on 3 May”).

Race report emails (only if you subscribe)

If you sign up, we keep your email address to send you a report after each race. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Emails are sent through Brevo. Your address is kept until you unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Notifications (only if you turn them on)

If you allow notifications, your browser gives us a push address (a long code from Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft) and the notification types you chose. We use it only to send those notifications. Turning notifications off in the app or in your browser stops them; you can also ask us to delete the record.

Kept on your device

The app saves a few settings in your browser (light or dark mode, your notification choices) and keeps its own files so it opens fast and works offline. These stay on your device and are not sent to us.

The AI and race data

Predictions are written by Google’s Gemini AI from public race information (results, standings, session times, weather). No information about you is sent to the AI. Race data comes from Jolpica, OpenF1 and Open-Meteo; your browser contacts some of them directly, so they see your IP address, as any website does.

Services we use

  • Vercel hosts the site.
  • Supabase stores the predictions, the usage counts, subscriber emails and push addresses.
  • Brevo sends the race report emails.
  • Google provides the AI and the fonts.

Each of them handles your IP address and technical data as part of delivering their service.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email ahmoudy365@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. The site is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will be announced in the app.
